01 — OUR STORY

A Quiet Corner for Book Lovers

Thornfield Library is a free digital library project designed to make discovering books easier and more enjoyable.

We bring together books from different genres, authors, and literary worlds in one organized space. Whether you are looking for a classic novel, a mystery, or a new fantasy world, our library gives you a place to begin.

Our goal is simple: to create a comfortable and accessible reading environment where stories can be discovered, saved, and enjoyed.

02 — OUR PURPOSE

Why We Created Thornfield

We wanted to create a simple digital space that brings books and readers closer together.

The project focuses on organization, accessibility, and a pleasant user experience. It is also an opportunity for our team to apply what we have learned about web development and build a complete website together.

Thornfield Library is an educational project and is provided as a free digital library, not an online bookstore or paid subscription service.
