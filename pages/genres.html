The directory

Five doors, five worlds

Every genre keeps its own weather. Choose a door below to enter a room with its own light, its own ornaments and its own three books — the same library, five different atmospheres.

Fifteen books in total, three per room. Seven volumes can be read online in public-domain editions; the remaining eight are catalogued with honest availability notes. Subgenre chips on each portal jump straight to a filtered shelf.
