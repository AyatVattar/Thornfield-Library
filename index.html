What this library is

A reading room, not a shop

Thornfield began as a student project with one stubborn rule: a library should ask nothing of its readers. There is no checkout, no subscription and no borrowing queue. You walk in, you choose a shelf, and you read what the law allows us to offer.

The collection is deliberately small — fifteen books, chosen to stand for five very different literary worlds. Seven are public domain in the editions we carry and can be read online; eight are still under copyright and are listed with accurate notes rather than pirated text. Your favourites, reading statuses and bookmarks are kept in this browser alone.

Our one stubborn rule

Why this library exists

Free, and staying that way

No prices, no carts, no subscriptions, no lending. The library is an educational project, and reading in it will never cost anything.

Only legal texts

We reproduce public-domain editions verbatim and nothing else. Where a book is still protected, we say so plainly instead of copying it.

Your shelf stays with you

Favourites, statuses and bookmarks live in your browser’s localStorage. No account, no cloud, no tracking — and no sync to other devices.
